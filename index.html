Found a tagged item?

Thank you for helping! Items carrying one of these tags belong to someone who wants them back. Normally the QR code takes you straight to the right page — if you got here instead, enter the tag ID printed on the tag below the QR code.

The ID is the short code engraved under the QR code.

TagBack — a simple lost & found QR tag system. No accounts, no apps, no tracking.
